Stay in touch when the network drops.

GoDark is group messaging designed for times when internet or mobile service is unavailable. Nearby phones can carry encrypted messages over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi; supported LoRa radios may extend the path.

THE IDEA

A message can move with the phones that meet.

GoDark is designed around short-range links first. It does not need to send your group's message through our account service.

01

Write for your group

A message is intended for a group and encrypted on the sending phone. The exact privacy guarantees depend on the app build and its tested implementation.

↗
02

Nearby phones may carry it

Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi links can let phones exchange and forward encrypted packets when compatible devices come within range.

↗
03

Other links can extend the path

Supported LoRa radios can add distance. When internet is available, public relays may help messages travel farther; that path is optional.

↗
04

A group member receives it

A message arrives only if devices, radio links, relays and the recipient's app cooperate. There is no guaranteed delivery time or service level.

↗

WHEN IT MAY HELP

Useful before the signal disappears.

Make a plan with your group, practise in ordinary conditions, and keep a backup way to reach local services.

A / 01

Blackouts & outages

Keep a group channel ready when mobile networks or home internet are unreliable.

A / 02

Preparedness groups

Coordinate with family, neighbors or a volunteer team during a planned practice or disruption.

A / 03

Check-ins & useful places

The app is designed to support safety check-ins and sharing useful places. Never assume a check-in or location has arrived unless you can verify it.

PROJECT STATUS

IN DEVELOPMENT

Honest about the gaps.

GoDark is an active development project, not a finished or certified emergency communications product. Some Android radio features and cross-device behavior have not been verified on real hardware. Coverage depends on the radio, operating system, nearby devices, configuration and environment.

Try only with people who understand the limits. Keep ordinary communication options available and do not rely on the app for urgent rescue, medical, safety or evacuation decisions.

ACCOUNT & DATA

Offline chat should not depend on an account.

Accounts are for optional cross-device settings and consented service features—not for routing group messages. The account and telemetry service is still being prepared for this preview. See what it is designed to collect and how long it is intended to keep it.
