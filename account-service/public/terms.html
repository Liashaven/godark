These draft terms cover the GoDark preview and the planned account service. The operator's legal identity, contact details and governing jurisdiction are not configured, so this is not ready to serve as a final public contract.

Important: Terms and conditions cannot guarantee that a person or project will not be sued. This document is not legal advice and does not replace review by a qualified lawyer in the jurisdiction where GoDark will be offered.

1. The service and its limits

GoDark is an in-development group-messaging application intended to help compatible nearby devices exchange encrypted message packets when ordinary internet or mobile service is unavailable. Bluetooth, Wi‑Fi, supported LoRa radios and optional internet relays may be used when available and configured.

GoDark is not an emergency-response, medical, safety, evacuation or rescue service. Message delivery, radio coverage, encryption behavior, availability and compatibility are not guaranteed. Messages can be delayed, lost, duplicated, exposed by a compromised or misconfigured device, or fail to reach their recipient. Keep established communication methods available and contact local emergency services for urgent help.

2. Development status and acceptable use

The application and account service are under active development. Some Android radio behavior and real-device compatibility have not been verified. The service may change, be interrupted or be unavailable without notice. Do not rely on this preview for critical decisions or store information that must remain available.

Use the service lawfully, respect other people's privacy and do not attempt to disrupt, exploit or gain unauthorized access to the app, accounts, infrastructure or communications. You are responsible for protecting your device and choosing a unique password.

3. Accounts and consented data

An account is optional for local offline group messaging. When registration is enabled, GoDark collects an opaque account ID, email address, chosen display name, salted password hash, account creation and last-login dates, accepted Terms/Privacy versions, optional-consent choices and change times, and active-session client type/creation/expiry metadata. The database stores one-way hashes of session and anti-forgery tokens, not their raw values. Account exports include non-secret session metadata but omit password and token hashes. Account information is kept while the account exists; deletion removes active account records and linked telemetry within 30 days.

Only with a separate opt-in, GoDark may collect usage feature names and event timestamps linked to the account (deleted after 12 months) or diagnostics consisting of app/device model, OS/app version and a limited crash category/hash linked to the account (deleted after 90 days). Security records may include IP address and sign-in/security events; website requests may also include a browser user-agent and request time, retained for 30 days. The account service does not collect message contents, group codes/keys, contacts, check-in contents or precise location. Backup copies are intended to expire within 90 days, but the provider limit has not been verified, so no backup-expiration promise is made here. See the Privacy Policy for details and choices. Sign-up is not available in this preview until retention and provider backup requirements are verified.

4. Privacy and communications

The account service is intended not to collect message contents, group codes or keys, contact lists, safety-check-in contents or precise location. Do not submit such information to account, diagnostics or analytics forms. Local deletion of an account cannot erase copies already stored on other devices or independent relays.

6. Changes, suspension and termination

The operator may change, suspend or stop the preview or account service. You may stop using GoDark and delete an account when the service provides that control. Data retention after deletion follows the Privacy Policy; local app data and message copies on other devices may remain.
