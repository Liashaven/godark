# GoDark Terms of Service — DRAFT

Last updated: November 16, 2026. These draft terms require qualified legal review before public launch. They are not legal advice and are not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific terms.

## Service and optional account

GoDark is intended to support messaging when internet or mobile service is unavailable. An account is optional. Offline messaging remains usable without signing in. The account service is not configured in this source snapshot, and account features are unavailable in this build.

## Delivery is not guaranteed

Messages may be delayed, lost, duplicated, unavailable or delivered to only some group members. Devices, radios, local networks, internet access, third-party relays, operating systems and power may fail or restrict delivery. Encryption does not guarantee that a recipient will keep information confidential, that a device is uncompromised, or that a message will be delivered. Users are responsible for verifying important information through an appropriate channel.

## Not an emergency service

GoDark is not an emergency dispatch, rescue, medical, security or life-safety service. Do not rely on it as the sole way to call for help, report a hazard, locate another person or coordinate an evacuation. Check-ins and location information can be stale, imprecise, unavailable or wrong. Contact local emergency services and use other reliable methods where possible. The on-device AI can be inaccurate and is not professional medical or emergency advice.

## User responsibility and lawful use

Use the app only where lawful and do not use it to threaten, harass, impersonate, unlawfully surveil or otherwise harm others. You are responsible for the information you send and for deciding whether to share public messages, check-ins, locations, photos, invites or account details. Information already received by another phone or an independent relay may remain available even after you delete a local copy or account.

## Legal consequences

Using GoDark, encryption, anonymity features, offline messaging or an optional account does not change applicable law or guarantee any particular legal outcome. Laws vary by location and circumstance. Do not treat the app or these draft terms as legal advice, and do not assume that use of the app provides immunity from legal process or liability.

## Account security and deletion

If an account is enabled, keep its credentials secure. Account deletion is intended to be completed within 30 days. It does not erase local mesh data, copies already delivered to other users, or data held by independent third-party relays. Provider backups are intended to expire within 90 days, but that has not been verified and is a launch blocker; this draft makes no promise about backup expiration.

## Availability and changes

The app and any future account service may be changed, suspended or unavailable. The present source snapshot has no configured public account API origin. The operator, governing law, dispute terms and verified support channel have not been configured. Qualified counsel must review and complete these terms before public launch.

Operator identity and support contact: **not configured**. No support email or mailing address is designated by this draft.
